# Decapitator (Soundtoys)

**What it is (plain English):** the analog destroyer → *five circuits of
modeled analog saturation, from console warmth to fully torn speaker.
Where Saturn is a surgical multiband lab, Decapitator is one glorious
knob that says "more attitude."*

**When we reach for it:** drum bus glue-with-teeth, making a DI bass or
plain 808 angry, vocal presence without an EQ boost (Style A + Tone),
and the **Punish button** moment when a drop element is 90% there but
too polite.

**Key controls:** Drive (the knob), Style (A = tape/console warm, E =
British console bite, N = Neve-ish thick, T = triode shred, P = pentode
chaos — *A for warmth, E for presence, T/P for violence*), Tone
(dark↔bright), Low/High Cut filters (pre-drive → *shape what gets
destroyed*), Mix (parallel destruction), Output, and Punish (+20 dB of
input, instantly).

**Macro rack:** `AGT Decapitator` — Drive, Mix, Tone, Style (per the
plan).

**Agent access:** macro rack; direct params via `param_dump` →
`decapitator.params.json`.

**Gotchas:** Drive raises level a lot — enable Auto (output compensation)
before comparing, or louder wins every A/B. On the drum bus, keep Mix
around 30–50% (parallel) so transients survive. Punish is a performance
gesture: automate it for one bar of a fill, don't leave it on.
